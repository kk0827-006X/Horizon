QuadRF, a 4x4 MIMO software-defined radio (SDR) tile integrated with a Raspberry Pi 5, now functions as a real-time RF camera that can detect drones and visualize WiFi signals through walls. This open-source platform democratizes advanced RF sensing, enabling hobbyists and researchers to explore previously expensive or classified technology, with implications for privacy, security, and wireless network diagnostics. The QuadRF uses a phased-array radio with an FPGA board for picosecond-level timing, enabling beamforming and advanced signal processing, and is powered by a Raspberry Pi 5.

**Background**: RF sensing uses radio waves to detect objects or movements, similar to radar but at lower frequencies. WiFi signals can pass through walls, and by analyzing their reflections, it's possible to image scenes behind obstacles. QuadRF extends this concept with a multi-antenna SDR that can pinpoint RF sources in real time.
