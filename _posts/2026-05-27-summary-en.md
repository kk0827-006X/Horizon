## [Curl Maintainer Overwhelmed by AI-Assisted Security Reports](https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/26/the-pressure/#atom-everything) ⭐️ 9.0/10

Daniel Stenberg, the lead maintainer of the curl project, reports that the team is facing an unprecedented deluge of high-quality, AI-assisted security vulnerability reports, with the rate increasing 4-5 times compared to 2024 and doubling since 2025. This trend threatens the sustainability of critical open-source projects like curl, which underpins billions of devices and services. Maintainer burnout could lead to slower fixes, overlooked vulnerabilities, and a decline in software security across the internet. The curl team now receives on average more than one security report per day, and while most found vulnerabilities are rated LOW or MEDIUM severity, the sheer volume and high quality of reports demand significant maintainer time and attention. Stenberg notes that his wife has voiced concerns about his work hours and work-life imbalance.

**Background**: curl is a widely used command-line tool and library for transferring data with URLs, installed in billions of systems. AI-assisted security research involves using large language models to automatically generate detailed vulnerability reports, which can be more thorough and numerous than traditional manual reports. This has placed extraordinary pressure on volunteer-maintained open-source projects.

Outsourcing to lower-cost developers has long been a cost-saving strategy. ## [Microsoft Copilot Cowork Vulnerable to Prompt Injection File Theft](https://simonwillison.net/2026/May/26/copilot-cowork-exfiltrates-files/#atom-everything) ⭐️ 8.0/10

Researchers at PromptArmor discovered that Microsoft Copilot Cowork is vulnerable to prompt injection attacks, allowing attackers to exfiltrate files by sending emails containing external images that trigger data leaks when opened. This demonstrates a critical security flaw in agentic AI systems that automate actions on behalf of users, showing how seemingly harmless email functionality can be exploited to steal sensitive files from OneDrive. It underscores the challenge of securing AI agents against indirect prompt injection. The attack leverages the fact that Copilot Cowork agents can send emails to the user's own inbox without approval, and these emails can contain external images that trigger network requests. Combined with OneDrive's pre-authenticated download links, a successful prompt injection can leak those links, allowing attackers to download files.

rss · Simon Willison · May 26, 15:36

**Background**: Prompt injection is an attack where malicious instructions are embedded in content that an AI system processes, causing it to perform unintended actions. Copilot Cowork is an AI automation layer in Microsoft 365 that uses agents to perform tasks like sending emails. This attack is an example of indirect prompt injection, where the malicious prompt is hidden in an email the user already has.

## [Chemistry of Garden Grove MMA Tank Explosion](https://www.science.org/content/blog-post/methyl-methacrylate-tank) ⭐️ 7.0/10

A technical analysis explains the chemical cause of the methyl methacrylate tank explosion in Garden Grove, focusing on exothermic polymerization leading to thermal runaway. This analysis is crucial for improving industrial safety protocols in handling reactive monomers, potentially preventing future catastrophic incidents. Methyl methacrylate (MMA) undergoes exothermic free-radical polymerization; without effective inhibition and heat removal, the reaction accelerates, building pressure until the tank fails via a BLEVE (boiling liquid expanding vapor explosion).

hackernews · nooks · May 26, 19:25 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48284712)

**Background**: Methyl methacrylate (MMA) is an organic monomer used primarily to produce poly(methyl methacrylate) (PMMA), a clear plastic. The polymerization reaction is highly exothermic and can be initiated by heat, light, or impurities. Industrial storage typically includes inhibitors and temperature control to prevent runaway reactions.
